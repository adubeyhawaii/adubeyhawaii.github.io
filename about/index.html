About

Background, education, and the things I’m into.

I’m a senior at The Harker School in San Jose, CA, graduating in 2027. I’m a Mehta Scholar and Venture Analyst for the school’s $10M Venture Investment Initiative, where I research alumni-founded startups and pitch investment verdicts to our Investment Committee.

This past summer I was a Venture Analyst at G2C Ventures in Palo Alto, working on deal and LP sourcing, diligence, and investment reporting. I wrote six investment memos, built the firm’s intern onboarding program as its first in-person hire, and advised a portfolio company on strategy and partnerships.

Outside of finance I lead speaker events at Oeconomia, Harker’s economics club, and captain the Varsity Men’s Water Polo team. I’ve refereed youth soccer since 2023, taught kids to swim, and once used a Julius Caesar soliloquy to explain corporate communication to engineering faculty in Raipur, India.
